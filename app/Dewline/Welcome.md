# Welcome to Dewline

Dewline edits Markdown files where they already are. Open your notes folder or a repo, and its notes show in the sidebar.

- Open a folder: ⌥⌘O
- Find a note: ⇧⌘O
- New note: ⌘N

When git, a sync app or an agent changes a note, Dewline marks each change so you can keep or revert it. ⌘-click to try it: [Show me a change](dewline://demo)

To have your notes on iPhone, keep their folder in iCloud Drive or a git repo and edit them there in Files, iA Writer or Working Copy. Dewline marks those edits when you're back.

This note is Dewline's own, kept outside your folders. Type anywhere. It saves as you go.
